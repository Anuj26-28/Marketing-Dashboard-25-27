Overall Profile Ranking Methodology

To provide a fair, objective, and transparent profile evaluation for corporate placement, each student is assigned an Overall Composite Score out of 100 Points based on four vital pillars:

1. Academic Consistency Max 35 Points

Evaluates longitudinal academic performance across secondary, senior secondary, and graduation degrees:

  • 10th Standard % (10 pts): Scaled linearly from 50% (0 pts) to 100% (10 pts).
  • 12th Standard % (12.5 pts): Scaled linearly from 50% (0 pts) to 100% (12.5 pts).
  • Graduation % (12.5 pts): Scaled linearly from 50% (0 pts) to 100% (12.5 pts).

2. Prior Corporate Work Experience Max 25 Points

Rewards professional maturity, lateral adaptability, and proven workplace track records:

  • Freshers: Baseline of 6 points for academic focus and quick learning agility.
  • 1 to 6 Months: 14 points (early professional exposure).
  • 7 to 12 Months: 18 points (solid organizational understanding).
  • 13 to 23 Months: 22 points (proven execution and lateral competency).
  • 24+ Months: 25 points (maximum score, ideal sweet spot for MBA lateral placement).

3. Summer Internship Depth & Impact Max 25 Points

Measures practical corporate performance during MBA summer tenures:

  • Primary Internship Baseline: 12 points for completion of summer project.
  • Dual Internship Multiplier: +6 points for completing a second verified internship.
  • Extended Duration (≥3 Months): +4 points for long-term project engagement.
  • Reputed Corporate Recruiter & Role Scope: Up to +3 points.

4. Professional Skills & Certifications Max 15 Points

Evaluates self-directed upskilling in contemporary tools and platforms:

  • Verified Certifications (up to 8 pts): NISM, Udemy, Forage, Infosys, Google, etc.
  • Core Tool Proficiency (up to 7 pts): MS Excel, Power BI, Generative AI, CRM, Python, SEO, Google Analytics.
Tie-Breaking Rule: Students with identical total scores are ranked by Graduation %, followed by Class 12th %.
